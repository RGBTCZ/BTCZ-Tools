# BTCZ Tools v1.4.5 ⛏️

**New: see exactly how many BTCZ went to miners today.**

## ⬇️ Download

Grab **`BTCZ-Tools.exe`** from the Assets below, double-click, done.
Already on v1.2.0+? The app will offer this update for you the next time you launch it. 🚀

## 🔧 What's new

- ⛏️ **Miner emission today** — a premium card in the **Network Explorer** that shows the exact amount of BTCZ paid to miners since local **midnight**. It counts every block actually found today (from the explorer, filtered by each block's timestamp against your local 00:00) and sums the real miner reward per block — the post-**Canopy 80%** share (**2,500** of the 3,125 BTCZ block reward; the remaining 20% goes to the community fund). The card also shows the ≈ value in your selected € / $ currency and the per-block reward.

Example: 176 blocks so far today → **440,000 BTCZ** emitted to miners.

Everything from v1.4.4 (shared-pool immature, remembered rigs, 60s auto-refresh) is included.
